## Apiculture regression

The 0.2.4 physical output is preserved as evidence. In 0.2.5, the runtime instructions contain no apiculture vocabulary or expected answers. Structural tests require only the general behavior: domain primitives and core workflows may be included when anchored; context-dependent harvest-like work remains optional unless its job is selected; market-like business expansion is not auto-included; an underspecified primary job becomes a high-impact unknown.

## Honest boundary

`STRUCTURAL EXPECTATION` is not `PHYSICAL MODEL OUTPUT`. The latter must be collected after installing the 0.2.5 unsigned IPA on the compatible iPhone.
