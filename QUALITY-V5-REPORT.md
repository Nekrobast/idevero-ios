# Foundation Models Quality V5

## Physical evidence entering V5

IDEVERO 0.2.6 physically preserved Domain Context, prevented the observed raw-token leakage, maintained scope discipline and cleared the bottom tab. Its remaining ceiling was epistemic: plausible sector patterns could be stated as facts, generic software findings could be domain-washed, downstream questions appeared before product direction, and some Local Expert labels remained English.

## Design

- Domain context items carry an internal epistemic status and decision relevance.
- Unsupported and low-relevance context is omitted.
- Case-dependent context is compiled as conditional, never as an established fact.
- Findings require material domain specificity, professional relevance and an anchored domain mechanism.
- Primary-job ambiguity suppresses dependent operational questions.
- Sparse input is allowed to produce sparse context; schema completeness is not rewarded.
- Display localization is independent from stable internal identifiers.

## Evidence boundary

Deterministic gates and fixtures validate structure, persistence, language mapping and question ordering. They are not reported as real Foundation semantic output. The next semantic judgment belongs to the physical 0.2.7 retest.
